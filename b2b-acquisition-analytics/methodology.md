# Methodology — B2B Customer Acquisition Optimization

## 1. Define the target customer and conversion

Clarify the intended B2B audience and distinguish a lead from a qualified prospect and a converted customer. Align Marketing and Sales on the definition of success.

## 2. Diagnose funnel performance

Review performance from impression through click, landing-page conversion, lead qualification, and customer acquisition. Segment results by channel, campaign type, audience, and search intent where permitted.

## 3. Improve traffic relevance

Use search-query analysis, negative-keyword practices, audience refinement, and campaign segmentation to reduce irrelevant traffic and improve the share of leads matching the target customer profile.

## 4. Optimize execution

Evaluate bidding approaches and budget allocation using downstream performance rather than click volume alone. Test creative and landing-page variations with clearly defined hypotheses.

## 5. Establish measurement discipline

Track lead volume, cost per lead, qualification rate, conversion rate, and acquisition cost against agreed targets. Document attribution assumptions and avoid treating correlation as proof of causality.

## 6. Run a continuous improvement loop

Maintain an experiment backlog, prioritize by expected impact and effort, review results, and translate findings into the next set of campaign changes.

## Reported outcome

The source notes report 300% lead growth, an 83% reduction in cost per lead, and acquisition costs 66% below target. These are reported program outcomes rather than independently verified causal estimates.
